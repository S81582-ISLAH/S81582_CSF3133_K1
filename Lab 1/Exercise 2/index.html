UMTea Cafe

UMTea Cafe is a cozy, study-friendly spot near UMT offering fresh teas, coffee, and light snacks for students, staff, and visitors. With friendly service and affordable prices, it's the perfect place to relax, meet up, or recharge. "Sip. Study. Repeat."

How to order from our menu

  1. Browse the menu and choose your item.
  2. Proceed to the counter to place your order.
  3. Specify your preferences (size, quantity, or options) to the staff.
  4. Confirm your order and total price.
  5. Make payment at the counter.
  6. Wait for your order number to be called.
  7. Collect your order and enjoy!

About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university in Malaysia, located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu. It was formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) and was upgraded to a full university in 2007. The university is situated near the coast and is well-known for its research in marine science, fisheries, and aquatic resources.

The campus is uniquely located near the South China Sea, providing students and researchers with direct access to marine environments for practical studies. UMT offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs across multiple fields, including science, technology, business, and social sciences, with a strong emphasis on sustainable development and environmental conservation.
